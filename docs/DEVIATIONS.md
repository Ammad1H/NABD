| D1 | 2026-11-08 | Pre-test, during model build (before tuning finished) | The NABD-EEQ grid gains the option `strata ∈ {pooled, level3}`. `level3` pools rolling-origin log-errors within three level strata (≤20, 20–200, >200 weekly admissions at the origin) instead of across all locations. The family ("empirical error quantiles pooled over locations") is unchanged. The option is chosen by the same 2023/24 tuning criterion. | A smoke test on a 2023/24 date showed pooled log-errors make large-state intervals implausibly wide (US 1%–99% range ≈ 690–15,900 when the last observation was 2,763), because small-count states dominate the log-error distribution. | **No.** No test-season (2024/25, 2025/26) score had been computed. |

| D3 | 2026-11-08 | Pre-test (added after freeze, before any test-season forecast was produced) | **Alert layer pre-specified for BUILD-SPEC G10 (US-A).** Surge onset for a location-season = the first week whose truth weekly rate is ≥ T per 100k for two consecutive weeks; T ∈ {2, 3, 5}, primary T = 3. Rates come from the hub `weekly_rate`; the population is observation/weekly_rate per location. **Forecast alert rule** (applied identically to NABD-ensemble and FluSight-ensemble): at reference date R, alert if the h=1 median ≥ T and the last observed week (R−7, vintage data) < T. **Census rule:** alert if the last observed week's rate ≥ 0.6·T and it is < T. The first alert per location-season counts. **Lead** = (onset week end − R)/7 weeks. **Sensitivity** = share of eligible onsets whose first alert has lead 0–4 weeks. **Premature** = first alert > 4 weeks before onset. **False alarm** = alert in a location-season with no onset. **PPV** = first alerts followed by onset within 4 weeks / all first alerts. Location-seasons already at or above T at the first reference date are ineligible and are counted. **Data latency:** report the input-to-issue lag and stale rounds. **Feed-dropout robustness:** re-run NABD-ensemble with inputs truncated by an extra d = 1 and 2 weeks, and report WIS degradation. These are secondary or diagnostic, not endpoints. The UAE alert part belongs to the UAE workstream. | G10 was added to BUILD-SPEC after the freeze. The rule is fixed here before any test-season forecast exists. | **No.** |
