The pain

A Monday decision, made with last year’s pattern

Every winter week, Abu Dhabi’s paediatric bed managers and public-health officers plan the next three weeks of RSV and flu. Their defaults are last week’s numbers and last year’s pattern, but UAE seasons don’t repeat, and no checkable forecast exists.

  • Flu peaked at 40.6% in week 42 (Oct 2025); the season before, a five-month plateau peaked in week 7 (Feb 2025)
  • The RSV peak moved between week 41 and week 47

2 · Source: WHO FluNet UAE sentinel positivity, our computation (NABD app, Season shape page)

Plan this winter from last winter and you’re wrong on timing and size. That’s not a crisis claim: DoH says this season looks like the last two. It’s the normal state of UAE respiratory seasons, and it recurs every year.
