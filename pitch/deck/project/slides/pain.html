The pain

A Monday decision, made with last year’s pattern

Every winter week, Abu Dhabi’s paediatric bed managers and public-health officers plan the next three weeks of RSV and flu from last week’s numbers and last year’s pattern. But timing and shape change from year to year, and no checkable forecast exists.

  • Flu: a months-long plateau peaking in week 7 (Feb 2025), then a sharp spike to 40.6% in week 42 (Oct 2025)
  • RSV peak height ranged 20% to 30% across 2022/23–2025/26

2 · Source: WHO FluNet UAE sentinel positivity, our computation (eval/deck_figures.py; NABD app, Season shape page)

Plan this winter from last winter and you can be wrong on timing and size. Some seasons do look alike: this season’s flu peak is close to last season’s. The point is that you can’t know in advance which shape you’ll get. That’s not a crisis claim: DoH says this season looks like the last two. It’s the normal state of UAE respiratory seasons.
