Evidence III · The caveats

The UAE’s own record, and what we don’t claim

Parity with “no change”

Trained only on earlier seasons, v1 vs last week carried forward = 1.002 for flu and RSV. That is why we keep a live forecast ledger.

Short history (post hoc)

UAE sentinel data start in 2022. With one training season, flu lost badly (WIS 4.75 vs 2.21). Gulf countries had flu history from 2015.

Vs last year’s pattern (post hoc)

With ≥2 training seasons, v1 beat the past-season pattern in 4 of 4 UAE folds (flu 0.61, RSV 0.68). With one, it lost both. Pooled CIs include 1.

More signals didn’t help

Multi-signal v2 was worse than v1: 1.059 (CI 1.016–1.111) under a locked protocol. It runs only as a challenger.
