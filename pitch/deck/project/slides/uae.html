The UAE’s own record, and what we don’t claim

Parity on the UAE’s own record

Trained only on earlier seasons, v1 vs “no change” = 1.002 for flu and RSV. That is why we keep a live forecast ledger.

Why, in our reading (post hoc)

UAE sentinel data start in 2022. With one training season flu lost badly (WIS 4.75 vs 2.21); with two or more, flu won both folds and RSV split. Gulf countries had history from 2015.

More signals didn’t help

Multi-signal v2 was worse than v1: 1.059 (CI 1.016–1.111) under a locked protocol. It runs only as a challenger.

The lead is on size, not onset

US median onset lead is 0 weeks for every rule, the CDC’s included. Onsets caught in time: census rule 28 vs NABD 17 of 51 (2024/25); NABD 29 vs 24 of 52 (2025/26). Diagnostic only.
