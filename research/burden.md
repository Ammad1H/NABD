# UAE / Abu Dhabi winter respiratory burden: evidence for the NABD pitch (G7)

Compiled 8 Nov 2026 by the evidence workstream. VERIFIED means the figure was read on the cited page today. Quotes are kept under 15 words. "Derived" means our own arithmetic on a verified figure.

## Ranked by usefulness for the pitch

### 1. RSV hospitalisations at Tawam Hospital, Al Ain (Abu Dhabi emirate), 2018–2022 — VERIFIED
- **Source:** Alamiri H.A., … Alsuwaidi A.R. *Epidemiological characterization and seasonality of respiratory syncytial virus in the United Arab Emirates: a five-year study at a tertiary care hospital, 2018–2022.* J Infect Public Health 2025;18(12):103007. DOI 10.1016/j.jiph.2025.103007. Record: https://research.uaeu.ac.ae/en/publications/epidemiological-characterization-and-seasonality-of-respiratory-s/
- **Figures:**
  - 15,326 children aged ≤5 tested (39,760 RSV tests).
  - **2,189 RSV-associated hospitalisations** at one hospital over 5 years.
  - Infants under 6 months had the longest stays, a **mean of 5.8 days**.
  - Annual RSV positivity swung from **2.9% (2020) to 16% (2021)**, then 6.1% in 2022.
  - 60.2% of the hospitalisations fell in 2021–2022.
  - Onset timing shifted during the pandemic and returned to pre-pandemic timing in 2022.
- **Derived:** about 440 RSV admissions a year on average at a single tertiary hospital, but very unevenly spread across years, which is exactly the planning problem.
- **Pitch use:** the anchor burden slide. It is Abu Dhabi-emirate data from a large public tertiary hospital (Tawam is a SEHA facility; the SEHA affiliation is common knowledge and was not re-verified in this pass). The year-to-year swing in positivity and hospitalisations is the "it is not the same season twice" argument.

### 2. Paediatric influenza at Tawam Hospital, 2012–2017 — VERIFIED
- **Source:** Al Ali et al. *Clinical spectrum, risk factors, and outcomes of children with laboratory-confirmed influenza…* Health Sci Rep 2021. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8596947
- **Figures:**
  - 1,392 children under 15 with lab-confirmed flu.
  - **33.5% admitted.**
  - **8% to PICU.**
  - 1.6% in-hospital deaths; 0.9% attributed directly to influenza.
  - Cases peaked **October to March**.
  - Only about 10% had been vaccinated.
- **Pitch use:** severity. Flu in children here is not trivial: one in three lab-confirmed cases was admitted, which justifies sizing beds rather than only flagging onset. The data is older (2012–17), so say so.

### 3. Paediatric RSV in Dubai insurance claims, 2014–2023 — VERIFIED (abstract)
- **Source:** Joury J., Al Kaabi N., … Farghaly M. *Retrospective Analysis of RSV Infection in Pediatric Patients… in Dubai (2014–2023).* J Health Econ Outcomes Res 2024;11(2). https://doaj.org/article/84cafb89b1744634800aad5def791c1d
- **Figures:**
  - 25,729 patients under 18 with RSV.
  - **22% of under-2s hospitalised.**
  - Mean stay about 4 days.
  - Fewer than 2% had known risk factors.
  - Reported cases rose **about 55% per year on average, 2014–2022**.
  - Total hospitalisation cost for under-2s was US$9.8M (median US$2,241).
- **Pitch use:** most admitted infants were previously healthy, so the load is population-wide and can't be predicted from risk lists. It also gives a cost anchor. Note it is Dubai and private claims only.

### 4. RSV season timing at Hatta Hospital, Dubai, 2022–2023 — VERIFIED
- **Source:** Salih S.S.A. et al. Dubai Med J 2024;7(1). DOI 10.18502/dmj.v7i1.17497. https://doaj.org/article/75791f142dda492b886f7c51da59ace7
- **Figures:**
  - Of 2,714 samples, 69% were positive for a respiratory pathogen.
  - RSV season ran **August–December, peaking in October**.
  - 21% of single-virus infections were hospitalised.
- **Pitch use:** independent confirmation that RSV peaks in autumn and early winter, consistent with the FluNet peak weeks 41–47.

### 5. Abu Dhabi flu campaign scale — VERIFIED
- **Source:** Abu Dhabi Media Office, 19 Jan 2026. https://www.mediaoffice.abudhabi/en/health/abu-dhabi-public-health-centre-vaccinates-350000-plus-people-as-part-of-2025-2026-annual-seasonal-influenza-campaign/
- **Figures:**
  - **More than 350,000 vaccinated** (Sep 2025–Mar 2026), up from 162,761 in 2022–23 (over 115% growth).
  - ADPHC said early-season flu admissions were mostly among unvaccinated people (no counts given).
- **Pitch use:** shows Abu Dhabi already invests heavily in the season. NABD helps the hospitals that absorb what prevention misses.

### 6. There are no public UAE flu case counts — VERIFIED
- **Source:** The National, 14 Jan 2026. https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2026/01/14/uae-influenza-season-flu-shot-preventive-tips
- **Quote (under 15 words):** "exact data for flu cases in the UAE is not available."
- The same article describes the dominant strain as H3N2 subclade K, with a Burjeel (Abu Dhabi) consultant saying flu-like outpatient visits were among the highest on record.
- **Pitch use:** the information gap NABD fills. Public signals are thin, so a calibrated forecast built from what is published (WHO FluNet) plus the hospital's own counts is valuable.

### 7. Abu Dhabi infant RSV immunisation — VERIFIED from a secondary source only
- **Source:** zavis.ai summary of DoH Circular No. 37 of 2026 (https://www.zavis.ai/intelligence/uae-respiratory-vaccine-season-2026), consistent with Sanofi Campus (https://pro.campus.sanofi/ae/respiratory-syncytial-virus/articles/rsv-prevention-guidelines-in-uae).
  - The primary circular is at https://www.doh.gov.ae/-/media/Feature/Resources/Circulars/2026/37--2026.ashx; our fetch tool was blocked by robots.txt, so it is UNVERIFIED first-hand.
- **Figures:**
  - Nirsevimab and clesrovimab were added to the childhood programme.
  - One dose per season; season window **1 Sep–1 Mar**.
- **Pitch use:** context only. Say "per DoH Circular 37/2026 as summarised by…" until the team opens the PDF in a browser.

### 8. The 2026–27 national campaign now covers RSV and pneumonia — VERIFIED
- **Source:** Khaleej Times, 16 Sep 2026. https://www.khaleejtimes.com/lifestyle/health/flu-campaign-2026-27-expanded-changes-explained
- **Figures:**
  - The campaign was expanded from flu to flu + RSV + pneumonia.
  - DoH Abu Dhabi's Director of Surveillance and Outbreak Response, Dr Mahra Alhosani, said activity so far is similar to the previous two seasons.
- **Pitch use:** policy has made RSV a named seasonal priority. **Caution:** the DoH quote says this season is normal so far, so do not pitch it as an "unprecedented surge". Pitch it as recurring, variable pressure.

## Not found (stated honestly)
- **No public Abu Dhabi ED winter volumes or bed-occupancy figures.** SEHA, PureHealth and Cleveland Clinic AD statements with numbers were not found. Media pieces quote individual clinic doctors (e.g. "almost 60%" of one Dubai clinic's patients had flu, The National, 11 Jan 2025), which is anecdotal and **not usable** as burden data.
- **No UAE national influenza hospitalisation estimate.** A modelling paper exists (Springer, Infect Dis Ther 2021, s40121-021-00534-3) but was not read in this pass; it is UNVERIFIED.
- **No adult RSV burden figure for the UAE.**

## Recommended pitch line (all VERIFIED)
> "One Abu Dhabi tertiary hospital recorded 2,189 RSV admissions of young children in five years, and yearly positivity swung more than five-fold. In Dubai, 1 in 5 infants with RSV was hospitalised, and almost none had a known risk factor. The UAE does not publish flu case counts. NABD turns what *is* published, plus a hospital's own counts, into a calibrated 1–3-week forecast."
